## Tone & Voice
1. **Authoritative but Accessible:** We speak like a Tier-1 financial institution that actually understands modern tech.
2. **Security First:** Every feature reveal and text block should reinforce the "Trustless" nature of the platform.
3. **Data-Driven:** Use metrics, IDs, and status codes to give the user a sense of absolute control and transparency.
